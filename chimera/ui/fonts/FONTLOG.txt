FONTLOG for UGG Display and UGG Mono
====================================

Chimera's own UI faces (first made for the Ultimate Gun Game, hence the
name), made for this project by Lucker Party contributors and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).
Sources: the build scripts in tools/fonts/ (tools/fonts/README.md).

Faces
-----
- UGG Display Black (900), Bold (700), Regular (400): wide, square-
  shouldered capitals whose bowls are squircles, open terminals cut 20
  degrees off vertical and diagonals at 70 degrees, both from the game's
  logo facets, and a lower case in the same construction. Tabular figures by
  default; `pnum` proportional figures, `zero` a slashed zero. Black
  carries `ss02`, the wordmark set (tighter, arm ends cut at 70 degrees,
  the plain A); `ss03`-`ss06` choose its A.
- UGG Mono Regular: the data monospace companion: squircle bowls, one
  advance, slashed zero by default (`ss01`: a plain zero; `ss03`: a one
  without its base), lower case on the same advance.

ChangeLog
---------
2026-11-15 v0.5
- Real lower case in all four faces (tools/fonts/lower.py: a-z, the
  accented Latin-1 lower case, dotless i, ae, eth, thorn, oslash; cv01 a
  single-storey a, cv02 an l without its tail; x-height 0.74 H, Mono
  0.76 H; descenders to -200 units, Mono -190). Kerning for lower-case
  and mixed-case pairs, accented letters kerning as their base. This is
  the shipped build now; UGG_FONTS_LOWER=0 builds the old caps-only
  mapping.
- The missing ASCII ($ < > ^ ` { } ~), a few Latin-1 symbols (· ÷ ¡ ¿
  « » ´ ¨ ¸) and the accented capitals of Latin-1 (À-Ý, Æ, Ð, Ø, Þ, Ÿ)
  plus ß, so names such as "Zoë" no longer fall back to another face.
- Line metrics unchanged (Display 850/-150, Mono 820/-160): descenders
  reach below the line box, so the kit's sentence role sets its lines
  1.3 em tall.
- Existing capitals, figures, advances and kerning unchanged.
- The faces moved to chimera/ui/fonts/: every app's UI text and Bevy's
  default font use them.

2026-11-13 v0.4
- Black added (with the wordmark set ss02 and the A alternates ss03-ss06).
- The faces committed with the game (apps/ultimate_gun_game/fonts/), TTF
  only.

2026-11-13 v0.2-v0.3
- Display Bold and Regular, Mono Regular; squircle bowls, kerning.
