FONTLOG for UGG Display and UGG Mono
====================================

The Ultimate Gun Game's own faces, made for this project by Lucker Party
contributors and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).
Sources: the build scripts in tools/fonts/ (tools/fonts/README.md).

Faces
-----
- UGG Display Black (900), Bold (700), Regular (400): wide, square-
  shouldered capitals whose bowls are squircles, open terminals cut 20
  degrees off vertical and diagonals at 70 degrees, both from the game's
  logo facets. Caps only (lower case maps to capitals). Tabular figures by
  default; `pnum` proportional figures, `zero` a slashed zero. Black
  carries `ss02`, the wordmark set (tighter, arm ends cut at 70 degrees,
  the plain A); `ss03`-`ss06` choose its A.
- UGG Mono Regular: the data monospace companion: squircle bowls, one
  advance, slashed zero by default (`ss01`: a plain zero; `ss03`: a one
  without its base).
