FONTLOG for UGG Display and UGG Mono
====================================

The Ultimate Gun Game's own faces, made for this project by Lucker Party
contributors and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).
Sources: the build scripts in tools/fonts/ (tools/fonts/README.md).

Faces
-----
- UGG Display Black (900), Bold (700), Regular (400): wide, square-
  shouldered capitals whose bowls are squircles, open terminals cut 20
  degrees off vertical and diagonals at 70 degrees, both from the game's
  logo facets. Caps only as shipped (lower case maps to capitals; the
  lower-case draft builds with UGG_FONTS_LOWER=1). Tabular figures by
  default; `pnum` proportional figures, `zero` a slashed zero. Black
  carries `ss02`, the wordmark set (tighter, arm ends cut at 70 degrees,
  the plain A); `ss03`-`ss06` choose its A.
- UGG Mono Regular: the data monospace companion: squircle bowls, one
  advance, slashed zero by default (`ss01`: a plain zero; `ss03`: a one
  without its base).

ChangeLog
---------
2026-11-15 v0.5
- The missing ASCII ($ < > ^ ` { } ~), a few Latin-1 symbols (· ÷ ¡ ¿
  « » ´ ¨ ¸) and the accented capitals of Latin-1 (À-Ý, Æ, Ð, Ø, Þ, Ÿ)
  plus ß. As shipped, accented lower case maps to these capitals, so
  names such as "Zoë" no longer fall back to another face.
- A draft lower case for all four faces (tools/fonts/lower.py: a-z, the
  accented Latin-1 lower case, dotless i, ae, eth, thorn, oslash; cv01 a
  single-storey a, cv02 an l without its tail; kerning for lower-case and
  mixed-case pairs). Not in the shipped files yet: build it with
  UGG_FONTS_LOWER=1.
- Existing glyphs, advances and kerning unchanged.

2026-11-13 v0.4
- Black added (with the wordmark set ss02 and the A alternates ss03-ss06).
- The faces committed with the game (apps/ultimate_gun_game/fonts/), TTF
  only.

2026-11-13 v0.2-v0.3
- Display Bold and Regular, Mono Regular; squircle bowls, kerning.
